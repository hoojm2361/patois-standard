**# Why this matters**



**Deepgram transcribes Jamaican Patois with roughly 30ÔÇô40% word error**

**rate. Whisper base is worse ÔÇö above 50% on accented speech. The**

**bottleneck is not the model; it's the training data.**



**To fine-tune a model that actually works for Patois, we need**

**consistent labels. Right now, every diaspora writer spells Patois**

**differently. That's a feature of our community, not a bug ÔÇö but it**

**means an ASR model can't learn "this audio = this text" reliably.**



**This group exists to change that, once, in a way the community**

**actually consents to. Not to police how people write in casual**

**settings. Not to override Cassidy/JLU. Just to document a spelling**

**convention that can serve as a training-data reference, and to build**

**the first Patois corpus that is:**



**- Spelled consistently enough to be useful for training**

**- Documented publicly so anyone can use it**

**- Contributed with consent and credited to the speakers**
